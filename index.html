About Me

Hi! I’m Jake, a 2025 Mechanical Engineering graduate and EIT from Merrimack College, currently working at Quest Global as a Gas Turbine Controls Engineer. I’m interested in robotics, control systems, and their real-world applications. My robotics experience includes implementing an Extended Kalman Filter in MATLAB Simulink for sensor fusion on a skid-steer mobile robot. I also served as a research assistant studying Human-Robot Interaction, exploring library patrons’ reactions to Pepper, a social robot, with customized story-reading scripts.

I have experience teaching STEM topics as a CRLA-certified tutor and enjoy creating intuitive explanations for complex concepts. I’m always exploring ways to combine software, math, and hardware to solve challenging problems. I’m currently seeking PhD programs for Fall 2026 in robotics, autonomous systems, and controls, where I can continue learning, conducting impactful research, and eventually mentoring the next generation of roboticists.

Recent Projects

  • Senior Design: Odometry and Sensor Fusion – Built a skid steered robot with tracking wheels, a gyroscope, and a camera for position tracking. Implimented an Extended Kalman Filter that took the Odometry system (tracking wheels, gyroscope) as the input and used the camera as a global reference for the update stage. Variance in update measuement adapted for motion blur. Presented in poster format at Merrimack College Research and Creative Achievement Conference 2025.
  • Research: Human Robot Interaction – Programmed a commercial social robot to performatively read stories to library patrons. Designed an experiment deploying the robot into a local library to tell stories to people, gathering data through an optional survey post-interaction. Conference paper in progress, presented locally in poster format at Merrimack College Research and Creative Achievement Conference 2025. Submitted to IMECE: Engineering Innovation for a Resilient Future 2025 in poster format.
