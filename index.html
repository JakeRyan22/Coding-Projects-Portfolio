About Me

Hi! I am Jake, a 2025 Mechanical Engineering graduate from Merrimack College. I'm passionate about robotics, control systems, and automation. My experience includes implementing an Extended Kalman Filter in Simulink to perform sensor fusion for a skid-steer mobile robot. The EKF accounted for coordinate frame rotation during state propagation and corrected gyroscope drift using a global heading reference. I designed the system to dynamically adjust measurement uncertainty based on inputs, improving performance across varying motion conditions. I enjoy creating intuitive explanations for complex topics, and I’m always exploring new ways to combine software, math, and hardware to solve real-world problems. I'm currently seeking opportunities in robotics and autonomous systems where I can continue learning and building impactful solutions.

Projects

Here are a few cool things I've built:

  • Senior Design: Odometry and Sensor Fusion – Built a skid steered robot with tracking wheels, a gyroscope, and a camera for position tracking. Implimented an Extended Kalman Filter that took the Odometry system (tracking wheels, gyroscope) as the input and used the camera as a global reference for the update stage. Variance in update measuement adapted for motion blur. Presented in poster format at Merrimack College Research and Creative Achievement Conference 2025.
  • Research: Human Robot Interaction – Programmed a commercial social robot to performatively read stories to library patrons. Designed an experiment deploying the robot into a local library to tell stories to people, gathering data through an optional survey post-interaction. Conference paper in progress, presented locally in poster format in Merrimack College Research and Creative Achievement Conference 2025. Submitted to IMECE: Engineering Innovation for a Resilient Future 2025 in poster format.
